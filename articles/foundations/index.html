Foundations

The method, the arithmetic and the reasons behind everything else on the site.

Everything else on this site rests on a few ideas, and these articles set them out. How much of the planet's water is actually available to people, and where it is. How a water footprint is counted, and what green, blue and grey water mean. Why a number on a product is a fact about the farm that made it and not a verdict on the person who bought it.

They are the method behind every figure here, written so that a reader with no background in water can follow the sums and check them.

They are also where the site says what it is for: an educational stream from inside the water industry, without the hooks, the guilt or the slogans, for people who would rather understand a number than be told what to feel about it.
